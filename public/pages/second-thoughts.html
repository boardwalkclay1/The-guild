What Is Trading?

Trading is the act of buying and selling financial instruments — stocks, options, futures, currencies — with the goal of profiting from price movement.

At its core, trading is simple:

• You identify where price is likely to move. • You define your risk. • You execute with discipline.

But in reality, it feels like stepping into an arena with a dragon that has eaten thousands before you. Every click is a swing of your sword. Every mistake has teeth.

What Are Options?

Options are contracts that give you the right — but not the obligation — to buy or sell a stock at a certain price, within a certain time.

In simple terms:

• Options let you control more value with less capital. • They can multiply gains — and losses — very quickly. • They are powerful, dangerous, and not a toy.

How Trading and Options Connect
