Live Market Overview
These are the main “dictators” of risk and sector flow. Use the buttons under each chart to pull up detailed explanations and heatmaps in the intel panel below.
Bitcoin (BTCUSD)
Bitcoin is the **crypto risk barometer**. BTC Up → risk-on, altcoins strengthen, liquidity increases. BTC Down → risk-off, miners weaken, volatility rises.
NASDAQ (Tech & Growth)
NASDAQ is the **tech and innovation engine**. NASDAQ Up → tech leadership, growth appetite, risk-on rotation. NASDAQ Down → rotation into safety, pressure on high‑beta names.
S&P 500 (SPX)
The S&P 500 is the **broad market scoreboard**. SPX Up → broad strength, institutional confidence. SPX Down → broad weakness, risk-off.
Dow Jones (DJI)
The Dow tracks **blue‑chip industrial strength** and economic backbone.
Russell 2000 (RUT)
Russell 2000 is the **small‑cap risk gauge** — early signal for risk appetite.
Volatility Index (VIX)
VIX is the **fear gauge** — how violently prices are expected to move.
US Dollar Index (DXY)
DXY is **global liquidity pressure** — strong dollar often weighs on risk assets.
10‑Year Yield (TNX)
The 10‑year yield is **macro gravity** — higher yields pressure growth and tech.
Crude Oil (CL1!)
Oil is **energy + inflation** — impacts transports, airlines, and consumer costs.
Gold (XAUUSD)
Gold is **macro safety + inflation hedge** — reacts to fear, rates, and currency moves.
