Even Numbers in the Market

The Guild treats even numbers as visible psychological levels — places where the whole world is watching.
Why Even Numbers Matter

Price is not just math — it’s human behavior. Traders, algorithms, funds, and retail all tend to cluster orders around clean, simple levels: $10, $20, $50, $100. These are easy to see, easy to remember, and easy to talk about.

Because of that, even numbers often become natural magnets and walls:

• Price will often gravitate toward an even number.

• Once there, it may stall, bounce, or reject from that level.

• Large orders, stop losses, and take profits are frequently stacked there.

Most of Your Trades Will Come From Them

As you grow in the Guild, you’ll notice a pattern: most of your best trades will start at or around an even number. Not because of magic — but because that’s where liquidity, attention, and emotion pile up.

When you combine even numbers with:

• Support and resistance

• Candlestick signals

• Volume and context

…you get high‑probability strike zones that you can see even without a full chart.
