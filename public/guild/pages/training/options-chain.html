The Options Chain – Guild Training Simulation

In the Guild Simulator, the options chain is your control panel. This is where you practice reading contracts, understanding how price moves, and recognizing which contracts react the most when the underlying stock moves.

Everything on this page is for training and education only. You are not placing real trades here — you are learning how to read the interface you will see on real platforms like Webull, Robinhood, and Chase.

Accessing Options – Training Flow

In real apps, you usually:

1. Search the stock (ticker) 2. Tap “Options” or “Trade Options” 3. Choose expiration date 4. Choose strike price 5. Choose call or put

In the Guild Simulator, you will practice this flow repeatedly until it is automatic.
