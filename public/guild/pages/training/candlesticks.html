🕯️ Candlestick Mastery

The Guild reads candles like language. Every wick, every body, every gap is a sentence in the story of price.

Below are the core formations you must know — not as trivia, but as signals on the battlefield.

Core Single Candles
🔨
Hammer
Bullish reversal • Buyers reclaim control
›

Structure: Small body near the top, long lower wick.

Story: Sellers pushed down, buyers slammed price back up.

Guild Note: A hammer at support is a battle cry.
🌠
Shooting Star
Bearish reversal • Buyers exhausted
›

Structure: Small body at bottom, long upper wick.

Story: Buyers tried to break out — sellers crushed them.

Guild Note: A shooting star at resistance is a warning flare.
⚖️
Doji
Indecision • Balance between forces
›

Structure: Open ≈ Close, long wicks.

Story: Neither side could dominate.

Guild Note: A doji is a question. The next candle is the answer.
🚀
Marubozu
Full conviction • Trend strength
›

Structure: Full body, no wicks.

Story: One side dominated from open to close.

Guild Note: Marubozu candles are momentum flags.
