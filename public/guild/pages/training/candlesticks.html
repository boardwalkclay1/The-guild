🕯️ Candlestick Mastery

The Guild reads candles like language. Every wick, every body, every gap is a sentence in the story of price.

These are not trivia patterns — they are decision tools. Each one tells you who is winning the battle: buyers or sellers.

Core Single Candles
🔨
Hammer
Bullish reversal • Buyers reclaim control
›

Structure: Small body near the top with a long lower wick and little to no upper wick.

Story: Sellers drove price down hard, but buyers stepped in and forced price back near the highs of the candle. That shift from heavy selling to strong buying is what makes the hammer a powerful bullish signal.

Where it matters most: At or near a support level, after a down move. A hammer in the middle of nowhere is just noise — a hammer at a key level is intent.

Guild Note: A hammer at daily support is the market whispering, “Enough selling.”
🌠
Shooting Star
Bearish reversal • Buyers exhausted at the top
›

Structure: Small body near the bottom with a long upper wick and little to no lower wick.

Story: Buyers pushed price up aggressively, but sellers slammed it back down before the candle closed. That failed push higher shows exhaustion from buyers and aggression from sellers.

Where it matters most: At or near resistance, especially after a strong run up. A shooting star at a major level is often the first sign that the party is over.

Guild Note: A shooting star at a key level is a flare in the sky — pay attention.
⚖️
Doji
Indecision • Balance between forces
›

Structure: Open and close are nearly the same, with wicks above and/or below.

Story: Neither buyers nor sellers could take control. Price moved, but it ended right back where it started. This is the market pausing, thinking, and waiting for the next push.

Where it matters most: After a strong move up or down, a doji can signal that momentum is slowing and a reversal or consolidation may be near.

Guild Note: A doji is a question mark. The next candle is the answer.
🚀
Marubozu
Full conviction • Pure momentum
›

Structure: Full body with almost no wicks — price opened at one end and closed at the other.

Story: One side dominated from start to finish. A bullish marubozu shows relentless buying; a bearish marubozu shows relentless selling.

Where it matters most: As a continuation candle in a strong trend, or as a breakout candle through a key level.

Guild Note: Marubozu candles are momentum flags — they tell you who is charging.
Multi‑Candle Formations
🟩🟥
Bullish Engulfing
Reversal • Buyers overpower sellers
›

Structure: A small red candle followed by a large green candle that completely engulfs the prior body.

Story: Sellers were in control, then buyers stepped in with enough force to erase the prior candle and close above its high. That shift in control is what makes this pattern a strong bullish reversal signal.

Where it matters most: At support or after a downtrend, especially when volume increases on the engulfing candle.

Guild Note: A bullish engulfing at a key level is the market saying, “We’re done going down.”
🟥🟩
Bearish Engulfing
Reversal • Sellers overpower buyers
›

Structure: A small green candle followed by a large red candle that completely engulfs the prior body.

Story: Buyers were in control, then sellers hit the market hard enough to wipe out the prior candle and close below its low. That is a clear sign that control has shifted from buyers to sellers.

Where it matters most: At resistance or after an extended move up, especially when volume spikes on the engulfing candle.

Guild Note: A bearish engulfing at the top is the market slamming the door shut.
🌅
Morning Star
Bullish reversal • Dawn after darkness
›

Structure: Three candles: 1) A strong red candle, 2) A small candle (often a doji or small body) that gaps down, 3) A strong green candle that closes into or above the first candle’s body.

Story: Sellers were in full control, then momentum stalled, then buyers took over with strength. It’s the story of a downtrend losing steam and a new uptrend beginning.

Where it matters most: At major support zones, especially on higher timeframes.

Guild Note: The Morning Star is the Guild’s “dawn” pattern — darkness, pause, then light.

📈
Breakouts
When candles punch through levels
›

Breakouts happen when price finally pushes through a level that has been holding it back. Strong candles (often marubozu‑like) with volume are the Guild’s preferred breakout signals.

Look for clean closes above resistance or below support, not just quick wicks.

Guild Note: A real breakout is conviction, not a random spike. Watch the candle body and the volume.
