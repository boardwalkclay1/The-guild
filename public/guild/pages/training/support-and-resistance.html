Support & Resistance

Support and resistance are the backbone of price movement. Support is where price tends to stop falling. Resistance is where price tends to stop rising.

These levels are where decisions are made — where buyers and sellers fight. Knowing these levels gives you a map of where price is likely to react.

Support

Support is a price level where buyers consistently step in. When price returns to support, you are watching a battle: Will buyers defend it again, or will sellers finally break through?

Resistance

Resistance is where sellers consistently overpower buyers. When price approaches resistance, you are watching for: Rejection, reversal, or a powerful breakout.
