Support & Resistance

These are the **battle lines** of the market ⚔️ Where buyers defend… and where sellers strike.

🟦 Support ▼

Support is the **floor** beneath price — the level where buyers step in with strength.

🟥 Resistance ▼

Resistance is the **ceiling** above price — the level where sellers push back with force.

📈 Trendline Support ▼

Trendline support shows buyers stepping in **higher each time**, forming an ascending base.

🏛️ Long‑Term Resistance ▼

Long‑term resistance is a **historic ceiling** — a level the market respects for months or years.

🔻 Rising Wedge (S&P) ▼

A rising wedge forms when support rises but resistance stays flat — pressure builds until it breaks.

🔄 Resistance → Support Flip ▼

When resistance breaks, it often becomes support — a powerful confirmation of trend strength.
