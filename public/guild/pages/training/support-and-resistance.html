Support & Resistance

Arcane Levels of the Market

In the Guild, we do not guess where price might move — we read the **ancient levels** it respects. Support and resistance are the **etched lines** where buyers and sellers have clashed for years. ⚔️

Learn these levels, and you stop trading like a wanderer. You begin trading like an **initiated member of the Order**.

🜂 Support — The Floor of the Realm ▼

Support is the **floor** beneath price — the level where selling pressure finally exhausts and buyers step in with force. Each time price returns to this level and holds, the floor becomes more **respected** by the market.

At support, you are not just looking at candles. You are watching **defense** — traders, funds, and algorithms all choosing the same zone as a place to buy.

Guild Insight:
The strongest support levels are not drawn by indicators — they are carved by **repeated reactions** over time.
🜁 Resistance — The Ceiling of Pressure ▼

Resistance is the **ceiling** above price — the level where buyers begin to hesitate and sellers grow bold. Each rejection from resistance is a sign that this level is guarded by **heavy supply**.

When price approaches resistance, you are watching for one of two outcomes: a **clean rejection** back down, or a **decisive breakout** that rewrites the structure.

Guild Insight:
A breakout without volume is a whisper. A breakout with volume is a **declaration**.
🜄 Trendline Support — The Rising Path ▼

Trendline support is where each low forms **higher than the last**, creating a rising diagonal floor. This shows buyers are not just defending — they are **advancing**.

When price pulls back into a rising trendline and holds, it often becomes a **high‑probability continuation zone**.

Guild Insight:
A trendline is not sacred on its own. It gains power when it aligns with **horizontal support** or a **key level from the past**.
🜃 Long‑Term Resistance — The Ancient Ceiling ▼

Long‑term resistance is a level that has rejected price across **months or years**. These are the levels where entire market cycles have turned.

When price returns to a long‑term resistance, you are not just trading a line — you are trading against the memory of every trader who was trapped there before.

Guild Insight:
Long‑term resistance broken with conviction can mark the **beginning of a new era** for that asset.
🜏 Rising Wedge — The Squeezing Trap ▼

A rising wedge forms when support rises but resistance stays flat or rises slower. Price is being **squeezed** into a corner — energy builds, but space disappears.

Many traders get comfortable inside a wedge. Guild members do not. We know wedges often resolve with a **sharp break** once the structure can no longer hold.

Guild Insight:
In a rising wedge, watch how price behaves near the **upper boundary**. Weak pushes into resistance often foreshadow the break.
🜍 Resistance → Support Flip — The Level Ascends ▼

One of the most powerful concepts in the Guild: when resistance is broken and then **respected as support**, the level has **ascended in rank**.

This flip confirms that sellers have lost control of that zone, and buyers now defend it. It is a classic sign of **trend continuation**.

Guild Insight:
The cleanest flips often come after a breakout, a pullback, and a **calm retest** — not a wild, emotional spike.
