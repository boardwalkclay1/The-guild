Support & Resistance

Arcane Levels of the Market

In the Guild, we do not guess where price might move — we read the **ancient levels** it respects. Support and resistance are the **etched lines** where buyers and sellers have clashed for years. ⚔️

🜂 Support — The Floor of the Realm ▼

Support is the **floor** beneath price — the level where selling pressure finally exhausts and buyers step in.

🜁 Resistance — The Ceiling of Pressure ▼

Resistance is the **ceiling** above price — the level where buyers hesitate and sellers grow bold.

🜄 Trendline Support — The Rising Path ▼

Trendline support shows buyers stepping in **higher each time**, forming an ascending base.

🜃 Long‑Term Resistance — The Ancient Ceiling ▼

Long‑term resistance is a level that has rejected price across **months or years**.

🜏 Rising Wedge — The Squeezing Trap ▼

A rising wedge forms when support rises but resistance stays flat — pressure builds until it breaks.

🜍 Resistance → Support Flip — The Level Ascends ▼

When resistance breaks and becomes support, the level has **ascended in rank**.
