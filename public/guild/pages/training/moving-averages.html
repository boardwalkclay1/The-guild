Moving Averages

Moving averages smooth out price action and reveal the true direction of the market. They remove noise and show you the underlying trend.

When a shorter moving average crosses above a longer one, it forms the Golden Cross — a powerful sign of strength. When it crosses below, you get the Death Cross, a warning of weakness.

Guild Master Notes:
• The 20 EMA shows short‑term momentum
• The 50 SMA shows medium‑term trend
• The 200 SMA shows long‑term direction
• Crossovers matter most when volume confirms
Common Mistakes:
• Trading every crossover blindly
• Using too many moving averages
• Ignoring trend context
• Entering before confirmation
