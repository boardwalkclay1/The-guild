Look Left

The market never forgets. Every bounce, every rejection, every battle between buyers and sellers is recorded on the chart. When you want to know where price might react next, you don’t guess — you look left.

Looking left means scanning past price action to find where the market has reacted before. These reactions reveal the true support and resistance levels across all timeframes.
